A bright, smiling profile photo

Pick a clear photo of your face in natural light, ideally facing a window. A real smile and eye contact with the camera feel friendly instantly, and that's exactly what viewers are looking for. 📸

Skip dark, far-away or heavily filtered photos, and anything that shows where you live or work. A simple, calm background lets your face be the star of the picture.

A username people remember

Go for a short username that's easy to type and say, so viewers can search for you after the live. A cute nickname beats your full real name: it protects your privacy and sticks in people's minds. ✨

Avoid long strings of numbers and odd symbols. Say it out loud: if it feels easy to you, your followers will love using it in the chat.

A short bio that says who you are

In two lines, say what makes you special and when you go live. Example: “Baking, laughs and good vibes 🍰 | Live every night at 9 pm | Come say hi!” Viewers love knowing what to expect.

Mention one clear passion: singing, drawing, cooking, languages. You'll attract people who share your interests, and they're usually the ones who turn into loyal followers. 💜

A live title that makes people tap in

Your live title is your invitation. Instead of “Hi”, write something that sparks curiosity: “Guess-the-song challenge 🎶” or “Let's bake an easy dessert together”. A clear title brings in people who love your topic.

Change the title to match each day's plan, but keep a recognizable style (same emojis, same tone) so your followers spot you in a second in the live list.

Keep your profile fresh and consistent

Update your photo and bio every few weeks, especially when your schedule changes or you add a new segment. A fresh profile tells viewers you're active and here for them. 🔥

Most important of all: be consistent. When a follower reads “every night at 9 pm” and really finds you live at 9, you build trust that keeps them coming back again and again.

Common questions

Do I need to use a real photo of myself?

A clear photo of your face usually attracts more followers, but privacy comes first: never share your address, workplace or school, and feel free to use a nickname.

How often should I update my bio?

Whenever your schedule changes or you add a new segment, or simply every few weeks to keep your profile fresh.
